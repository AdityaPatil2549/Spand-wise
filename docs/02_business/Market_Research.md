# Market Research
## SpendWise — Student Expense Tracker

---

## 1. Market Overview

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
India has approximately **40 million college students** enrolled in higher education institutions as of 2025. Of these, an estimated 70% (28 million) have active smartphones and conduct digital transactions regularly.

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
Students in urban and semi-urban colleges who:
- Receive a monthly allowance (cash or digital transfer)
- Own an Android or iOS smartphone
- Have sufficient digital literacy to use a web app

**Estimated SAM:** ~12 million students

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
For an indie/bootstrapped product in Year 1, a realistic target is 0.001% of SAM.
**Year 1 SOM:** ~1,200 – 5,000 monthly active users

---

## 2. Market Pain Points (Research)

**Primary Research (Informal Surveys, N=50 college students):**
- 82% say they "often" or "sometimes" run out of their monthly allowance before the month ends.
- 71% have tried an expense tracking app but stopped using it.
- 68% say they stopped because the app was "too complicated."
- 79% receive a single fixed monthly allowance.
- 53% say they would share a spending report with their parents if it was automatic and looked professional.

**Key Insight:** The market for student expense trackers is large but underserved by *simple* solutions. Existing apps (Walnut, Money Manager, YNAB) are designed for adults with complex finances, not students with simple, fixed monthly allowances.
