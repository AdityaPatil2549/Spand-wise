# Target Audience
## SpendWise — Student Expense Tracker

---

## 1. Primary Target Audience

**Who:** Indian college students aged 18–25 years old.

**Where:** Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 Indian cities. Attending engineering, medical, commerce, arts, or management colleges.

**Device:** Primarily Android smartphones (70%), secondarily iPhone/iOS (30%).

**Connectivity:** Mixed—reliable campus Wi-Fi in study areas, spotty mobile data in hostels and transit.

**Financial Reality:**
- Fixed monthly allowance from parents or scholarship (₹5,000–₹20,000/month).
- No credit history or credit cards (mostly debit cards and UPI/cash).
- No financial advisor or budgeting app experience.
- Knows roughly how much they spend, but not the details.

---

## 2. Psychographic Profile

| Attribute | Characteristics |
|:---|:---|
| **Motivation** | Wants to be financially responsible but finds existing tools too complex. |
| **Frustration** | Runs out of money before the month ends, especially after a few impulsive buys. |
| **Values** | Independence, peer status, self-improvement, tech-savviness. |
| **Behavior** | Checks phone 100+ times/day; uses Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp daily. Prefers apps that "just work." |
| **Aspiration** | Save for a trip, laptop, or concert ticket; prove to parents they're responsible with money. |

---

## 3. Secondary Target Audience

**Segment B: Working Professionals (Entry-Level, 22–28)**
- Recent graduates in their first job with a fixed salary
- Similar to students: fixed income, want to understand where their money goes
- May have slightly more complex needs (rent, EMIs)
- **Potential v2.0 expansion target**

**Segment C: International Students**
- Indian students studying abroad, or students in other developing markets
- Similar fixed-allowance model, different currency
- **Not targeted in v1.0** due to multi-currency complexity

---

## 4. User Segmentation for Marketing

| Segment | Size | Channel |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Hostel students | Large | College WhatsApp groups, hostels' notice boards |
| Day scholars | Large | College canteen, college fests, peer referrals |
| Engineering students | Very Large | GitHub, tech communities, college hackathons |
| Commerce/Finance students | Medium | Finance clubs, CA aspirant groups |
| Students on scholarship | Medium | Government scholarship portals, college welfare departments |

---

## 5. Anti-Audience (Who We're NOT Targeting)

- Salaried professionals with complex multi-account finances → Use a full-featured app like Money Manager
- Business owners tracking business expenses → Use Zoho Books or Tally
- Investment-focused individuals → Use Groww or Zerodha
- Users needing international multi-currency support → Not v1.0
