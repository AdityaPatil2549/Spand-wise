# Privacy Policy
## SpendWise

**Effective Date:** July 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 21, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

SpendWise ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the SpendWise expense tracking application ("the App").

**Summary (Plain Language):**
- We collect only what we need to run the app
- We never sell your data
- We never show you ads based on your data
- You can delete your account and all your data at any time
- Your financial data stays private

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

| Information | Why We Collect It |
|---|---|
| Name (from Google or entered by you) | To personalize your experience |
| Email address | For authentication and account recovery |
| Monthly budget amount | Core app functionality |
| Expense amounts | Core app functionality |
| Expense categories | To help you understand spending patterns |

- ❌ Bank account or credit card information
- ❌ UPI IDs or payment credentials
- ❌ Government identification (PAN, Aadhaar, etc.)
- ❌ Location data (GPS, IP-based)
- ❌ Contact list or address book
- ❌ SMS content
- ❌ Browser history
- ❌ Information about other apps on your device

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

1. **Provide the expense tracking service** — Store and display your expenses
2. **Calculate budget status** — Show your remaining budget and spending breakdown
3. **Generate reports** — Create monthly expense reports at your request
4. **Sync across devices** — Keep your data consistent across all your signed-in devices
5. **Send budget alerts** — Notify you when you're approaching or exceeding your budget (with your permission)
6. **Improve the app** — Analyze (anonymized, aggregated) usage patterns to make SpendWise better

**We do NOT use your data to:**
- Show you personalized advertisements
- Sell to third parties
- Create profiles for marketing purposes
- Make automated decisions that affect your financial standing

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Where Your Data Is Stored
Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) on servers in the **asia-south1 (Mumbai, India)** region.

### 4.2 Security Measures
- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3
- All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 (Google's default)
- Access to your data is controlled by Firebase Security Rules — only you can read or write your data
- Authentication is managed by Firebase Authentication (industry-standard JWT tokens)
- We do not have employees with routine access to your personal expense data

### 4.3 Data Backup
Your data is automatically backed up by Firebase. Backups are encrypted and stored for a maximum of 90 days.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

| Right | How to Exercise |
|---|---|
| **Access** | Your data is always visible in the app | 
| **Correction** | Edit any expense directly in the app |
| **Deletion** | Delete individual expenses or your entire account |
| **Export** | Download your data as CSV or PDF from the Reports section |

| **Objection** | Contact us at privacy@spendwise.app |

### Account Deletion
To delete your account and all associated data:
1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
2. Confirm by typing "DELETE"
3. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|

## 8. Children's Privacy

SpendWise is intended for users aged 13 and above (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with their data, please contact us at privacy@spendwise.app and we will delete it immediately.

SpendWise is designed for Indian students but is available globally. Your data is stored in Firebase's Mumbai (India) data center. If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

For GDPR requests, contact: gdpr@spendwise.app

## 10. Cookies and Local Storage

SpendWise uses:
- **Firebase Auth tokens** (stored in IndexedDB by Firebase SDK) — Required for login
- **Firestore offline cache** (stored in IndexedDB) — Required for offline functionality
- **App preferences** (localStorage) — Theme, notification settings

We do **not** use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.
