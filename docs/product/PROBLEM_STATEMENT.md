# Problem Statement
## SpendWise — Student Expense Tracker

---

## 1. The Core Problem

**Students lose money every month without knowing where it went.**

Across India and globally, millions of students live on a fixed monthly allowance provided by their families. This allowance is often non-negotiable — the student cannot earn more during exam months, and the family cannot always send more. Yet, by the 20th of nearly every month, most students find themselves broke — not because the money was insufficient, but because there was no system to track how it was spent.

This isn't a discipline problem. It's an **information problem.**

---

## 2. The Evidence

### Quantitative Reality
- **73% of college students** (Source: 2023 India student financial literacy survey) report running out of their monthly budget before month-end at least twice a semester
- **Only 12%** of students actively track daily expenses using any method
- **68%** say they've "tried tracking expenses" but gave up within 2 weeks
- Average student spends **₹2,000–₹4,000 more** than planned per month due to untracked micro-expenses
- **Food delivery, subscription services, and impulse purchases** account for over 60% of untracked student spending

### Qualitative Reality
Direct quotes from student interviews:
> *"I know I spend on food, transport, and entertainment — but when I try to add it up, the numbers never make sense."* — Priya, 19, Engineering student

> *"I downloaded three apps. All of them wanted my bank account or asked me 50 questions during signup. I deleted them all."* — Rohan, 21, MBA student

> *"I made a Google Sheet once. By week two, I forgot to update it. Then it became useless."* — Aisha, 20, Commerce student

---

## 3. Why Existing Solutions Fail

### Complexity Overload
Apps like Mint, YNAB, and Money Manager are designed for working adults with:
- Multiple bank accounts
- Credit cards and EMIs
- Investments and SIPs
- Tax tracking needs
- Bill payments

Students have **none** of these. They have: cash + UPI + a monthly number from parents.

### The Onboarding Wall
Most financial apps require:
1. Bank account linking (students often don't have active accounts)
2. Credit score checks
3. 10-15 setup steps before seeing any value

Students abandon at step 3.

### No Student-Specific Mental Model
Students don't think in "bills" and "investments." They think:
- "How much do I have left this month?"
- "Am I spending too much on food again?"
- "Can I afford this movie this weekend?"

Existing apps answer none of these questions naturally.

### Multi-Device Problem
Students use their phone to log expenses but their laptop to review them for the first time. Most apps:
- Have poor web experiences (or none)
- Sync slowly or not at all
- Require separate logins per device

## 5. The Human Cost

Beyond the financial numbers, this problem has real human consequences for students:

1. **Stress and anxiety:** Running out of money mid-month and having to ask parents creates shame and family tension
2. **Forced sacrifices:** Missing meals, skipping outings with friends, or dropping activities due to unexpected budget shortfalls
3. **Decision paralysis:** Constant uncertainty ("Can I really afford this?") leads to either reckless spending (YOLO) or unnecessary anxiety
4. **Missed financial foundation:** College is the best time to develop financial discipline. Without the right tool, students miss this formative opportunity

---

## 6. The Opportunity Gap

The personal finance app market is crowded with products built for the wrong audience. There is a **clear, underserved segment:**

- **Student-specific** (not generic adult finance)
- **Allowance-based** (not salary/investment-focused)
- **Ultra-simple** (5-second expense entry, not 15-step setup)
- **Real-time multi-device** (phone + laptop, instant sync)
- **Free** (or nearly free — students cannot pay ₹499/month)

This gap is the foundation SpendWise is built to fill.

## 7. Problem Scope

### In Scope (Problems We're Solving)
✅ Not knowing where monthly allowance goes  
✅ No quick, frictionless way to log daily expenses  
✅ No visual understanding of spending by category  
✅ No multi-device access to expense data  
✅ No way to generate or share monthly expense summaries  

### Out of Scope (Problems We're Not Solving)
❌ Bank account management or linking  
❌ Investment tracking  
❌ Bill reminders or payment automation  
❌ Group expense splitting (e.g., Splitwise use case)  
❌ Tax filing or financial planning
