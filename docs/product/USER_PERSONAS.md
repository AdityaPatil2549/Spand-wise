# User Personas
## SpendWise — Student Expense Tracker

---

## Persona 1: Priya — "The Overwhelmed First-Year"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  👩‍🎓 Priya Sharma                                            │
│  Age: 18  |  Engineering Student, Year 1                    │
│  Location: Pune (from Nagpur)                               │
│  Monthly Allowance: ₹8,000/month                            │
│  Primary Device: Android smartphone (₹12,000 phone)         │
│  Secondary Device: Shared college laptop                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background
Priya just moved out of her family home for the first time to attend an engineering college in Pune. She gets ₹8,000/month from her parents — just enough for hostel food, transport, and a few personal expenses. She's never managed her own money before and is overwhelmed by the new freedom.

### Goals
- Know exactly how much she has left each month without doing math in her head
- Stop getting surprised by an empty wallet on the 22nd
- Be able to show her parents a "money report" at month-end to justify her allowance
- Feel in control of her finances for the first time

### Frustrations
- "I tried a banking app but it asked for my PAN card and I didn't know what that was"
- "Spreadsheets are for my accounting class, not for tracking chai and lunch"
- "My phone storage is almost full so I need a lightweight app"
- "I forget to log expenses by evening — I need to log them right after spending"

### Behaviors
- Pays mostly via UPI (GPay), some cash for college canteen
- Checks her bank balance 3–4 times a week but doesn't track what she spent
- Shares phone with her roommate sometimes
- Uses WhatsApp, Instagram, and YouTube heavily — tech comfortable

### What SpendWise Needs to Do for Priya
- ✅ Zero-friction signup (Google login = 1 tap)
- ✅ Add expense in under 5 seconds (big buttons, simple flow)
- ✅ Show remaining budget prominently on home screen
- ✅ Work great on mid-range Android
- ✅ Month-end PDF she can share with parents on WhatsApp

### Quote
> *"If there's a quick way to just tap what I spent and on what, I'll actually do it."*

## Persona 2: Rohan — "The Conscious Tracker"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  👨‍💻 Rohan Mehta                                             │
│  Age: 21  |  MBA Student, Year 2                            │
│  Location: Mumbai                                           │
│  Monthly Income: ₹15,000 allowance + ₹8,000 part-time job  │
│  Primary Device: iPhone 14 + MacBook Pro                    │
│  Secondary Device: Office laptop (internship)               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background
Rohan is a second-year MBA student who is meticulous about his finances. He's done the Excel tracking before, but it's become burdensome. He wants analysis — he wants to know if his spending patterns are improving, not just what he spent.

### Goals
- See category-wise breakdowns (Food vs. Entertainment vs. Education)
- Compare this month to last month automatically
- Download a clean report for his personal records
- Have his expense data available on both his phone and Mac simultaneously

### Frustrations
- "I added 40 expenses to an app and then it crashed. Lost everything."
- "Why does every app require my credit card for 'advanced features'? I just want a chart."
- "I switch between my phone and MacBook constantly. I need real sync, not 'export and import'."
- "I want to see if I'm improving over time — not just the current month"

### Behaviors
- Logs expenses immediately at point of transaction
- Reviews monthly reports on laptop
- Pays via UPI, credit card, and cash
- Heavy app user — currently uses 3 different apps for different financial needs

### What SpendWise Needs to Do for Rohan
- ✅ Seamless phone + browser sync (real-time)
- ✅ Category analysis with month-over-month comparison
- ✅ Clean, exportable monthly report (PDF + CSV)
- ✅ Historical view (last 6 months)
- ✅ Fast, reliable, doesn't lose data

### Quote
> *"I want to open my laptop and immediately see what I spent on my phone 2 minutes ago."*

## Persona 3: Aisha — "The Reluctant Tracker"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  👩‍🎨 Aisha Khan                                              │
│  Age: 20  |  Design Student, Year 3                         │
│  Location: Hyderabad                                        │
│  Monthly Allowance: ₹6,000/month + occasional transfers     │
│  Primary Device: Android (OnePlus) + Windows laptop         │
│  Tracking History: Tried and quit 4 different apps          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background
Aisha knows she should track expenses but every app she's tried has felt like a chore. She's a creative student — visuals matter to her. She's tried Walnut (too SMS-dependent), Spendee (too complex), and a Notion template (abandoned after week 1). She'll try SpendWise but won't tolerate another complicated app.

### Goals
- Track spending without it feeling like work
- Understand her spending visually (charts > numbers)
- Not get overwhelmed with features she doesn't need

### Frustrations
- "I don't understand half the features in existing apps. I just want the basics done well."
- "The apps I tried were ugly. If it looks boring, I don't use it."
- "I hate it when apps send me 10 notifications a day."

### Behaviors
- Tends to forget to log expenses in the moment
- More likely to do a batch entry (logs 3–5 expenses at once at end of day)
- Responds well to beautiful visual design
- Gets frustrated quickly with slow or buggy apps

### What SpendWise Needs to Do for Aisha
- ✅ Beautiful, clean UI that feels premium (not clinical)
- ✅ Quick batch entry (can add multiple expenses fast)
- ✅ Visual category charts (pie/donut, not just tables)
- ✅ Minimal, non-intrusive notifications
- ✅ Fast performance (doesn't lag on mid-range phones)

### Quote
> *"If it looks like a bank app, I'm deleting it. If it looks like something I'd actually enjoy opening, I'll use it every day."*

## Persona 4: Dev — "The Budget-Conscious International Student"

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  👨‍🔬 Dev Patel                                               │
│  Age: 23  |  MS Student in the US (Indian student abroad)   │
│  Location: San Jose, CA (from Ahmedabad)                    │
│  Monthly Budget: $600 stipend + family support              │
│  Primary Device: iPhone + MacBook                           │
│  Key Need: Multi-device sync, category tracking             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background
Dev is on a tight budget as an international student. Every dollar matters. He needs to track spending meticulously but also needs to show his parents a monthly breakdown to justify how he's spending their hard-earned support.

### Goals
- Real-time sync between all devices (most important need)
- Monthly breakdown to share with parents in India via WhatsApp
- Clean PDF report with spending summary

### What SpendWise Needs to Do for Dev
- ✅ Cross-device real-time sync (his #1 need)
- ✅ Beautiful downloadable PDF report
- ✅ Works perfectly on both iOS and macOS browsers
- ✅ Fast, reliable, no data loss

## Anti-Persona: Marcus — "The Finance Power User"

```
❌ NOT our target user
```

Marcus is a 28-year-old working professional who wants to:
- Link all 3 bank accounts
- Track mutual fund investments
- Plan for tax savings
- Create complex budget rules

**Why SpendWise is NOT for Marcus:**  
SpendWise is intentionally not built for adults with complex financial lives. Marcus would find SpendWise too simple. That's a feature, not a bug.
