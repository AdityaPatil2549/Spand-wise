# Student Personas
## SpendWise — Student Expense Tracker

To design effective features, we target specific types of students. Each persona has different financial constraints and behaviors.

---

## Persona 1: "Hostel Haris" (The Independent Boarder)
**Profile:** 2nd-year engineering student living in a college hostel away from home.
*   **Income:** Fixed ₹8,000/month from parents on the 1st of the month.
*   **Fixed Costs:** Mess fees (prepaid), mobile recharge, Spotify student.
*   **Variable Costs:** Swiggy/Zomato (late-night study sessions), auto/cab to the city on weekends, movies.
*   **Pain Point:** "By the 20th of the month, I usually have ₹500 left and have to survive on Maggi. I don't know where it all goes so fast."
*   **SpendWise Goal:** Needs aggressive pacing warnings ("You're burning through money too fast") to stretch the budget to day 30.

---

## Persona 2: "Day Scholar Priya" (The Commuter)
**Profile:** 3rd-year commerce student living at home, commuting 15km to college daily.
*   **Income:** Variable. Parents give ₹200-₹300 every few days for travel and lunch.
*   **Fixed Costs:** None (parents cover rent, Wi-Fi, electricity).
*   **Variable Costs:** Metro/Bus tickets, college canteen lunch, stationery, shopping with friends.
*   **Pain Point:** "I don't get a fixed monthly allowance, so standard budgeting apps don't work for me. I just want to track how much I spend overall on travel vs. food."
*   **SpendWise Goal:** Needs a highly flexible budget setup (or the ability to just track expenses without a strict ceiling initially) and strong category analytics.

---

## Persona 3: "Hustler Rohan" (The Earning Student)
**Profile:** Final-year design student who freelances on the side.
*   **Income:** ₹5,000 from parents + ₹5,000 - ₹15,000 from irregular freelance gigs (Fiverr/Upwork).
*   **Fixed Costs:** Software subscriptions (Adobe CC, Figma), premium coffee shops (for Wi-Fi/working).
*   **Variable Costs:** Tech gadgets, online courses, socializing.
*   **Pain Point:** "My income fluctuates wildly. Sometimes I feel rich and buy a new keyboard, then I'm broke for 3 weeks waiting for a client to pay."
*   **SpendWise Goal:** Needs Cash Flow / Net Balance tracking (v2.0) and Goal Tracking (e.g., "Save ₹40k for a new MacBook").

---

## Persona 4: "Scholarship Sana" (The Meticulous Planner)
**Profile:** 1st-year medical student on a strict government scholarship.
*   **Income:** ₹6,000/month stipend. Highly rigid.
*   **Fixed Costs:** Textbooks (expensive), hostel, basic meals.
*   **Variable Costs:** Very little. Every rupee counts.
*   **Pain Point:** "I have absolutely zero margin for error. If I overspend by ₹500, I can't pay for my practical manuals next week."
*   **SpendWise Goal:** Needs the 50/30/20 rule, micro-budgeting per category (e.g., max ₹500 on leisure), and absolute data accuracy.

---

## Summary Implications for Design
1.  **Flexibility is Key:** The budget must accommodate both lump-sum allowances (Haris) and irregular top-ups (Priya/Rohan).
2.  **No-Shame Analytics:** Sana and Haris have very different spending capacities. Insights must remain objective and encouraging, never judgmental about the *amount* spent, only the *pattern*.
3.  **Speed Matters:** All personas track expenses on the go (in an auto, at the canteen). The entry flow must take under 5 seconds.
